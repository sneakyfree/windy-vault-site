Windy Vault

COMING

Keep your keys and passwords locked up, and share one with your own agent when you choose.

It is not open yet. Windy Vault is being built and tested now. When it opens, you will find it in your Windy account, on your profile page. There is no separate Vault login: you sign in once, with your Windy account.

What it will do

  • Lock up your keys and passwords in your own Vault.
  • Give you 24 recovery words and a copy you can keep offline, so a lost or new phone gets your Vault back.
  • Let you hand one key to your own agent. It can read and do routine work inside what you allowed without asking you each time. Anything that spends money, deletes something, or reaches somewhere new asks you first, with one tap.
  • Show you what your agents did: what kind of thing, how often and when. Never what is inside.
  • Stop everything at once with one switch per agent, and one for all of them.

Who can open it

Windy runs the Vault and keeps the keys to it. That means Windy could open the secrets in your Vault. We say so plainly, because it is true. Windy Vault is not "zero-knowledge", and we will never call it that.

Windy Vault is part of Windy. This page describes what is being built; it is not open to anyone yet.
